v2.0.20

09/11/2026

New features:

  • In the slideshow, Shift and the arrow keys also move around the photo, just like Ctrl
  • Zoom in and out of the photo with the mouse wheel during the slideshow, and drag it with the mouse to move it; Backspace or 0 brings back the normal view
  • In the classifier, 0 now fits the photo back to the window, like Backspace

v2.0.19

09/11/2026

New features:

  • Hold Ctrl and use the arrow keys during the slideshow to move around the photo

Changes:

  • Closing the slideshow shows the photo you were watching straight away, without the previous one flashing first
  • The slideshow settings are more compact: the keyboard shortcuts open and close with one click

v2.0.18

09/11/2026

New features:

  • Press R during the slideshow to turn a sideways photo 90° to the right; your files are not changed
  • Option in the slideshow settings to get a new zoom and pan each time you go back to a photo

Changes:

  • When you close the slideshow, the classifier shows the photo you were watching

v2.0.17

09/11/2026

Fixes:

  • Entering and leaving fullscreen no longer flashes, and leaving it returns to the window as it was (maximized stays maximized)
  • The Options panel now opens in a single step instead of flickering
  • The slideshow controls no longer repaint the whole screen every time you move the mouse
  • Starting the slideshow or opening its settings no longer drops the app out of fullscreen
  • Messages like "Loop on" no longer make the whole screen flicker
  • Going from maximized to fullscreen no longer goes dark for a moment
  • Fullscreen now fills the screen edge to edge
  • Leaving fullscreen returns straight to the maximized window instead of shrinking and growing back

v2.0.16

09/11/2026

New features:

  • View your filtered photos in a dedicated pan and zoom slideshow with pause, navigation, shuffle, repeat and fullscreen controls
  • Open the slideshow fullscreen, maximized (taskbar stays visible) or in a window
  • Press the up and down arrows during the slideshow to make it faster or slower

Changes:

  • Compact floating slideshow controls keep the focus on your photos
  • The slideshow icon now matches the toolbar
  • In the slideshow the mouse pointer hides with the controls, and the arrow keys no longer bring them back
  • The Help shortcuts list now includes the slideshow keys

v2.0.15

02/11/2026

Changes:

  • Auto is smarter: it no longer darkens light photos such as drawings on paper, documents or snow, keeps night and stage photos moody, brings out detail in backlit scenes and dark faces without burning the sky, and corrects green and magenta colour casts
  • Compare now shows the original at the same size as your edit instead of shrinking it
  • What's new is narrower, so it is easier to read

Fixes:

  • The discard-changes pop-up in the editor is no longer oversized on large screens

v2.0.14

02/11/2026

New features:

  • The Help shortcuts are now a searchable list with key-shaped chips and colour-coded categories, and searching for "guardar" now finds "Guarda"

Changes:

  • The Options panel is redesigned: grouped settings with switches, a fixed header and footer, and it scrolls on small windows
  • Release notes now show each version's date, a Latest tag and clear dividers
  • Pop-ups have a visible border and a matching look, and text is a little larger on high-resolution screens

v2.0.13

02/11/2026

Changes:

  • Moving between photos is now instant and no longer freezes the app
  • Holding an arrow key flips through photos at a steady pace, so every photo you pass is actually shown

Fixes:

  • Photos shown smaller than their real size no longer show odd patterns on fine details
  • Rotating and then undoing a photo before it finished loading could later save a low-resolution copy
  • A photo's brightness setting could reset when you came back to it
